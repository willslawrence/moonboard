Logbook

Export CSV makes a file of the logbook that is showing, a line a go. Import CSV reads a file laid out the same way and adds its goes to your own logbook. To bring an old logbook in: export first (an empty logbook gives a sample, the headings and a few example lines), put a line for each go under them, and import the file. The example lines are left out. A line needs a date (2026-10-12), the route's name as the list has it, and how it went: try, flash, 2nd go, 3rd go or 4+ goes. The time and the other columns may be left empty; the hold set, if empty, is the one on the wall. Goes come in locked. Several sends of a route in a day are all kept, and a go the logbook already has is not added again. Lock my entries locks every go of yours, so that none can be deleted by a slip.
MoonBoard scoring: 350 at 5+, 50 more for each grade above it, by the community grade (the one the climbers voted for), whichever grade this phone shows; then 53 more for a flash, 2 for a second go, 1 for a third. Benchmarks only, and only the first time you send a route.
Worked out from the goes in the logbook that is showing: the grades you have sent, how often first go, the route that took longest, the ones that got away, your days and your holds. Flash grade: the hardest grade at which half or more of the routes you have tried went first go. Edge grade: the hardest at which half or more went in the end, however many goes it took. Average send: the middle of all your sends. On your own logbook, a tap on any of the three opens a route of that grade you have not sent, on the hold set now on the wall. Next up counts what the next grade would take. Head to head puts you beside another climber. Share my story as a picture makes one picture of it all to send on. The more of your logbook is in here, the more it can tell. Import CSV brings an old logbook over.

Counted on the wall, a rest is a red light on the bottom row for each minute left, then START in big letters and the rim flashing twice. That is only while the route you have open is the one on the wall. If somebody lights another, the count stops and the wall is theirs: light yours again when your rest is up. The grade is left off the bottom row while the session is on.
On the board, tap a circle after each go. After the last one the rest counts down beside the wall. When it is up the phone pings and the screen flashes white. Tap the time to skip a rest.

First time here? Add it to your Home Screen

Add it to your Home Screen

Opened from its own icon the app fills the whole screen, with no browser bars round it.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari, not Chrome.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up. On a newer iPhone it can be under the three dots.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, leave it switched on. Then tap Add.

Your icon opens in the browser? Delete it and add it again this way.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones Install app), then Add.

On the picture of the wall

Five things a finger does there.

  • Two tapsThe picture fills the screen, for a closer look. Two more put the page back.
  • A short swipe downThe same: on to the full screen, and off it again.
  • A long swipe upLights the open route on the wall. It has to go far, so that it cannot happen by a slip.
  • A flick left or rightThe next route in the list, or the one before.
  • On the full screen, tap a holdIt is picked. Light the holds you pick, or find the routes that use them.

Points on the Sun board

A route scores once, the first time you send it: its V number plus one (V0 is 1, V4 is 5), and one more for a flash. Tries score nothing. These are the Sun board's own points and are not added to MoonBoard points.

Lately on the Sun board
Which wall

Both are at Will's. The Sun board has no lights. Its routes are made here: two taps on the picture, tap the holds, and save them as a route. Everyone who climbs it sees them. Nothing from the MoonBoard shows here, and nothing from here shows there.

Climbing as

The same name as on the MoonBoard. There are no passwords: a name is a name.

Background

For this phone only, and for both walls.

The map of holds

The outlines were drawn from photos by a program, and it gets some wrong. Stand at the wall and tap a hold it missed, an outline that is only tape, or one that is the wrong shape. Everyone has your fixes straight away, and the outlines are then redrawn properly from them. While you fix, the picture is the photo: a hold the map has missed is not in the drawing at all.

The picture

Two taps on the picture and it fills the screen; there a tap picks a hold. Two fingers zoom, one drags. A hold that has no outline can still go in a route: tap it while marking the route and a ring goes where you tapped.

Which wall

Both are at Will's. The Sun board has no lights. Its routes are made here: two taps on the picture, tap the holds, and save them as a route. Everyone who climbs it sees them. Nothing from the MoonBoard shows here, and nothing from here shows there.

Climbing as

The same name as on the MoonBoard. There are no passwords: a name is a name.

Background

For this phone only, and for both walls.

The map of holds

The outlines were drawn from photos by a program, and it gets some wrong. Stand at the wall and tap a hold it missed, an outline that is only tape, or one that is the wrong shape. Everyone has your fixes straight away, and the outlines are then redrawn properly from them. While you fix, the picture is a photo of one wall at a time: a hold the map has missed is only in the photo.

The picture

A finger across the picture turns your head, from the flat wall round to the overhang, and it stays where you leave it. Flat and Overhang in the picture's corners go back to those two views. Two taps and the picture fills the screen; there a tap picks a hold, and a swipe up or down, or the cross, goes back. Two fingers zoom: apart for closer, together for further off, until the whole wall is in sight. A hold that has no outline can still go in a route: tap it while marking the route and a ring goes where you tapped.

Logbook

Points on the Sun board

A route scores once, the first time you send it: its V number plus one (V0 is 1, V4 is 5), and one more for a flash. Tries score nothing. These are the Sun board's own points and are not added to MoonBoard points.

Log entry

Deleting works your tries, sends and points out again from what is left.
